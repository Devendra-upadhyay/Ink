सवालों से भरे मन को..

कुछ प्रश्न उत्तर नहीं चाहते,
केवल एक शांत हृदय चाहते हैं।

सवालों से भरा मन, बीते हुए समय की यादें, और उन उत्तरों की तलाश जो शायद कभी मिलें ही नहीं। यह कविता उन विचारों के बारे में है जो भीतर चलते रहते हैं, तब भी जब बाहर सब शांत दिखाई देता है।

A little about
the writer.

I am Devendra Upadhyay, the writer behind Independent Ink — a personal space for poetry, ideas and observations about life.

I write to give shape to thoughts that are difficult to express in everyday conversations. Through poetry and reflective writing, I explore time, human emotions, questions and the quiet details that often go unnoticed.

Independent Ink is an evolving collection of those thoughts — written independently, shared honestly and open to interpretation.

— Devendra Upadhyay
